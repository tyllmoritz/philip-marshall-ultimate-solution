        How it works

          At each step the solver builds several candidate solutions (different first moves, series or algorithms that place more pieces without disturbing finished ones) and keeps the one with the lowest movecount coefficient (MCC), an estimate of how hard the moves are to execute. The name comes from Philip Marshall's solution, the method it was first built for; this is an independent implementation, so move counts differ from his write-up.
